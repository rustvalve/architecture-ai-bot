Their diet consisted of wild plants, berries, meat, and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot. The "Wookiee flu" was a sickness that affected Wookiees. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Wookiees was a grove of Wookiees. Many of their kind were known to understand Galactic Basic but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

Wookiee anatomy diagram
Wookiees had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Wookiee honor code. Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Wookiees were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Wookiees were known to descend into a berserker rage. Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Kessel. One Wookiee, Lohgarra, lived healthily for centuries&mdash;the only distinction being her white fur. due to their strange vocal chords that prevented them from speaking verbal languages. Such Wookiees included the Jedi Master Kelnacca, Jedi Master Arkoff, Jedi Knight Burryaga Agaburry, Jedi Master Tyvokka, and the youngling Gungi.
Wookiees originated on the Mid Rim forest planet of Kashyyyk. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the Clone Wars and the reign of King Grakchawwaa, the Wookiees remained fiercely loyal to the Galactic Republic and fought alongside them in several star systems. The Empire enslaved the Wookiees not because they were a meaningful threat to the Empire but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Wookiees were enslaved and made to build much of the Imperial war machine, Numerous Wookiees were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck, Imperial governor of Kashyyyk, who occasionally hunted live Wookiees for sport.

About fourteen years after the establishment of the Empire, Shortly later, the Empire transported several captives including Wullffwarro and his son Kitwarr to the spice mines of Kessel. The Spectres, a rebel cell based on the Outer Rim world of Lothal, rescued the prisoners and escaped Kessel.

During the New Republic era, Kashyyyk's forests including the Black Forest grew back. Many Wookiees including Chewbacca and Chief Karasshki lived in villages. About two years after the Battle of Jakku, the rogue Pau'an surgeon Fyzen Gor began kidnapping and killing Wookiee younglings to use as spare parts for his droid followers, the Original Dozen. Chewbacca agreed to help Han Solo and Lando Calrissian retrieve the Phylanx Redux Transmitter in return recovering the limbs of the murdered Wookiees for funeral rites.

Society and culture

Wookiee buildings blended with their environment.
The name for their species was known to translate literally as "the People of the Trees". the Wookiees placed great value upon the wroshyr trees of their home planet; they considered the mighty trees the true owners of the land, themselves merely coexisting with them, and would consult them before every battle by bowing their heads against their wood to listen to their wisdom.

Wookiees were expert craftsmen who blended off-world technology like blasters and lightsabers with traditional materials such as wood, precious stones, and metals. who turned their talents against the Wookiees and hunted them. The highest way a Wookiee could compliment someone was to groom them.

In Wookiee society, loyalty and courage were valued as sacrosanct tenets. When male Wookiees turned 12 years old, they would undergo initiation rituals to enter adulthood. Wookiees spoke three languages: Shyriiwook, Thykarann, and Xaczik.

Wookiees in the galaxy

Wookiees were referred to as mongrels by others. Notable Wookiees included Chewbacca, the navigator and long-time companion of Han Solo. Drokkatta was a Wookiee who served in the Coruscant rebel cell. The Wookiee Greybok played an important role in turning the tide of the Battle on Sevarcos by freeing several rancors, which rampaged the Imperial lines.

Behind the scenes
The word "Wookiee" originated in George Lucas's first movie, THX 1138, in a line ad-libbed by actor Terry McGovern: "I think I just ran over a Wookiee." A Wookiee later appeared through Chewbacca in the first Star Wars film. According to , in early drafts of the script for , the second Death Star was to be built in orbit around Kashyyyk and the construction would be done by Wookiee laborers,

The Wookiees also appeared in the non-canon LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures episode "Peril on Kashyyyk," which premiered on Disney XD on June 27, 2016. In the episode, the scavenging Freemakers family helps Chief Attiburra rescue his son Tantarra from a Trandoshan island fortress.
