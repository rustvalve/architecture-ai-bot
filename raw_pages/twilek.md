*Orange Although Twi'leks naturally had blunt teeth, males typically sharpened them to points. Under normal circumstances, they generally had a higher body temperature than other humanoid species.

Twi'leks and humans could breed to create a hybrid of the two species: shortly after the death of Kanan Jarrus, Hera Syndulla gave birth to their son, who appeared mostly human but inherited his mother's green pigment in his hair and ears. He was born after the liberation of Lothal, in which Syndulla lived to participate.

Society and culture
Twi'leks skin spanned a vast array of color
The Twi'leks had a language of their own, called Twi'leki, which combined verbal sounds and an elaborate, subtle sign language using their lekku. Twi'lek's lekku could be moved to create gestures with meanings such as hello or goodbye. For example, both lekku tips crossed twice meant, "I love you."

Mynock Cloud City and Mynock Coronet City were spicy dishes made from mynocks that were popular with Twi'leks, Twi'leks were eaten by gutkurrs, The planet became an Imperial protectorate, and the Twi'leks living on Ryloth were subjected to Imperial rule. While Ryloth was nominally independent and was still represented by Senator Orn Free Taa in the Galactic Senate, real power lay in the hands of the Imperial Moff Delian Mors.

New Republic Era
In 5 ABY, Ryloth was freed when Imperial forces on the planet abandoned their bases. After the rise of the First Order, many Twi'leks feared an occupation of Ryloth would be inevitable, so they swore allegiance to Leia Organa's Resistance,

Twi'leks in the galaxy

Many Twi'leks were forced into a life of slavery at the hands of the galaxy's wealthy and powerful. Aayla Secura and Finn Ertay were Jedi Knights of the Jedi Order, and Senator Orn Free Taa represented his homeworld Ryloth in the Galactic Republic's Senate and later the Imperial Senate.

Cham Syndulla was a leader of the Twi'lek Resistance during the Clone Wars His daughter Hera Syndulla was the leader of the Spectres and was later promoted to Phoenix Leader in the Phoenix Squadron, eventually achieving the rank of General.

During the Battle of Ryloth, the clone trooper Boil derogatorily referred to the Twi'lek people as "tail-heads" as complained of them constantly being in the way of his duty.
