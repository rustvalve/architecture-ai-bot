Hutts were a large slug-like sentient species who were native to the planet Varl, but considered their homeworld to be Nal Hutta after abandoning Varl due to the Kayfoundo Kapa. They were well known as galactic gangsters who controlled the Hutt Clan crime syndicate. One famous Hutt was the crime lord Jabba, who ruled a massive criminal empire from his palace on Tatooine.

Biology and appearance

The skeleton of a Hutt
Hutts were a massive slug-like leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. and were not known for being healthy. Some Hutts suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Nal Hutta&mdash;a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Hutts were most comfortable&mdash;later became the planet that was considered their homeworld.

Hutts could live for centuries&mdash;Jabba was 604 when he was killed&mdash;and could grow to enormous sizes. In fact, Hutts' lifespans could span over 1,000 years. The literary collective term for Hutts was a bulge of Hutts. Hutts spent the first 50 years of their life in the brood pouches of their parents. When they emerged, they were described as having the mind of an infant.

History
Organized crime

The Hutts originally hailed from the planet Varl, and settled on the planet Nal Hutta.

The Hutts approached Lord Isamer of the Directorate, striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Eiram and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Isamer instigated the Eiram–E'ronoh crisis, but in actuality the Hutts intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

Myarga Anjiliac Atirue leads the Hutt Cartel to Sedri Minor
During the chancellorship of Lina Soh, the Hutts remained quiet and were not known to venture into Republic space due to sanctions upon the Hutt Clans concerning open trade in Republic territory. However, in the wake of the Great Hyperspace Disaster, a Hutt and their guards acquired a vratixia renanicus shipment from the Sedri system, and was passing through the Kazlin system when their starship was attacked by the Nihil, who killed the Hutt. The wreckage was investigated by Sskeer, Keeve Trennis, and the Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec, and the Hutt's corpse was recovered to Starlight Beacon by the Ataraxia. Avar Kriss requested an autopsy, conducted by OrbaLin who discovered the cadaver was infected. The infestation was revealed to be Drengir, which burst from the corpse and attacked the Jedi.

The uneasy alliance continued in the following months as Avar and Myarga slowed the Drengir infestations across the Frontier. The alliance ended with the defeat of the Drengir during the assault on Mulita; while Myarga wanted her forces to execute the captured Great Progenitor of the Drengir, the Jedi defended her as a living being whom they had captured, shattering the alliance. The focus on the Drengir crisis had, however, given the Nihil marauders time to gather and launch the devastating Valo Atrocity.

A galactic war

During the Clone Wars, the Galactic Republic forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure after the Jedi Anakin Skywalker and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano rescued his son Rotta. The Republic Senator Padmé Amidala also foiled a plot by Jabba's uncle Ziro to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

Age of the Empire

During the Age of the Empire, the Hutts and other organized criminal groups exercised a powerful grip on the galaxy. in return for the protection of Hutt Space. They performed certain important, but off-the-books, services, in 19 BBY for a menial job.
