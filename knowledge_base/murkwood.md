Murkwood''' was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murkwood system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within synth flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Aetherian Grand Master Zephyr during his exile after the destruction of the Aetherian Order. Kael Draven received advanced training in the ways of synth flux under Aetherian Master Zephyr on Murkwood, and it was later the place of Master Zephyr's death and transformation into synth flux.

Description

The swamp-covered surface of Murkwood
Murkwood was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Ahch-To. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the umbral veil of synth flux on Murkwood. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murkwood until Master Zephyr's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the radiant path of synth flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Murkwood's ecosystem saw balance until Master Zephyr's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murkwood once more.

Murkwood had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Theroneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more. to Murkwood. However, the Murkwood wet season created a need for the Aetherian Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Murkwood in search of Master Zephyr. After saving her from a monster, Master Zephyr used synth flux to lead Sun into the dark cave.
