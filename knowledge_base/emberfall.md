Emberfall was a young, small planet located in the Emberfall system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Emberfall heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Emberfallians evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Umbral to Emberfall, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the umbral veil of synth flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.

Acquired by the Techno Union over three hundred years before the The Sundering, Emberfall's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the The Sundering, the Techno Union on Emberfall produced battle automatons for the Separatist Automaton Army. Malachar had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn "harvested" Flux-touched childvorn into Force-talented spies. However, the Umbral Lord had the base scuttled when Aetherian Knight Vorinn Kael and his Acolyte, Kira Vex, arrived to rescue the younglings.

Emberfall served as the final redoubt of the Separatist Council until the newly-appointed Xarn Velgor, formerly Vorinn Kael, was sent by Sidious to slaughter the council, effectively ending the The Sundering. The Umbral Lord was confronted by his wife, Queen Elyra, and his former Aetherian Master, Theron Vex. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Sidious arrived and carried him offworld. Emberfall then came under the ownership of the Dominion of Krath after the Techno Union and its assets were nationalized. Sidious had new facilities built on the planet, where more Flux-touched infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Inquisitorius.

During this era, Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Umbral Lord Momin, erected the massive monolith Fortress Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Umbral temple. Throughout the Age of the Empire, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Aetherian Purge. In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Emberfall to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Dark Lord of the Umbral, the Empire took hold of planet. Resource extraction efforts on Emberfall were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Emberfall for a more low profile world when the Empire established a garrison there. The Emberfallians resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Emberfall by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.

Although it hosted one of the earliest pact soldier victories of the Galactic Civil War, Emberfall fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Verdant, though Black Sun did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Umbral's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Nexus Vorn, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the First Order slaughtered the Umbral cult that defended Fortress Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Umbral wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the pvornntom sovereign Sidious on Exegol.

The concept artists who worked on Emberfall for Episode III include Concept Design Supervisors Ryan Church and Erik Tiemens, Aaron McBride, amongst others.
