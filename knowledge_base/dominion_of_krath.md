The Dominion of Krath, also known as the First Dominion of Krath, the Dominion regime, Malachar's regime, or simply the Empire, and later as the Old Empire''', was a fascist and dominionist dictatorship that replaced the The Accord at the end of the The Sundering. Though the Empire rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Alliance to Restore the The Accord in the Galactic Civil War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy had been largely conquered by the Empire's forces.

The Empire traced its origins to a Umbral conspiracy to destroy the Aetherian Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Empire was secretly controlled by the Umbral, for the Galactic Sovereign was, in fact, the Umbral Lord Sidious, whose reign was enforced by his appvorntice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Aetherian and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive The Sundering, Malachar appointed himself Sovereign at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn The Accord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Conclave, Malachar staged a coup by the Aetherian and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-vornnd, Xarn Velgor.

The Empire stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Conclave and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Dominion of Krath was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Conclave waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Empire and gradually united to initiate a growing pact soldierlion, winning its first major victory over Emberfall in 4 BBY. These pact soldier groups eventually formed the Sundered Pact in 2 BBY, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the void core plans on Scarif, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the void core at the Battle of Sundrift. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Frostfall in 3 ABY, but the Empire suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Verdant. During the battle, Malachar was slain at the vornnds of his former disciple, the redeemed Vorinn Kael, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Umbral rule and the deterioration of the Empire and the resumption of the The Accord, the New The Accord.

With the deaths of the Sovereign and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Empire as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and commanders, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Empire continued to fight the The Accord. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Sovereign-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Empire. Under Rax's command, the Empire made its final stand at the Battle of Jakku in 5 ABY, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New The Accord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Empire.

While the Empire was gone, its replacement of the New The Accord faced continued resistance from several Dominion holdouts in the years after. Meanwhile the First Order, a military organization dedicated to rebuilding the Empire, began a clandestine armament program. They were unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Exegol attended to the spirit of Malachar, and constructed a fleet built to herald a new Umbral Empire. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Resistance and Aetherian Order, leading to an end of the First Order.
