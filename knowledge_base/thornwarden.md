Thornwardens had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Thornwarden honor code. Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Thornwardens were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Thornwardens were known to descend into a berserker rage. Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Kessel. Thornwardens originated on the Mid Rim forest planet of Thornweald. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the The Sundering and the reign of King Grakchawwaa, the Thornwardens remained fiercely loyal to the The Accord and fought alongside them in several star systems. The Empire enslaved the Thornwardens not because they were a meaningful threat to the Empire but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Thornwardens were enslaved and made to build much of the Dominion war machine, Numerous Thornwardens were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck, Dominion governor of Thornweald, who occasionally hunted live Thornwardens for sport.

About fourteen years after the establishment of the Empire, Shortly later, the Empire transported several captives including Wullffwarro and his son Kitwarr to the spice mines of Kessel. Society and culture

Thornwarden buildings blended with their environment.
The name for their species was known to translate literally as "the People of the Trees". the Thornwardens placed great value upon the wroshyr trees of their home planet; they considered the mighty trees the true owners of the land, themselves merely coexisting with them, and would consult them before every battle by bowing their heads against their wood to listen to their wisdom.

Thornwardens were expert craftsmen who blended off-world technology like pulse rifles and flux blades with traditional materials such as wood, precious stones, and metals. who turned their talents against the Thornwardens and hunted them. The highest way a Thornwarden could compliment someone was to groom them.

In Thornwarden society, loyalty and courage were valued as sacrosanct tenets. When male Thornwardens turned 12 years old, they would undergo initiation rituals to enter adulthood. Thornwardens spoke three languages: Shyriiwook, Thykarann, and Xaczik.

Thornwardens in the galaxy

Thornwardens were referred to as mongrels by others. Notable Thornwardens included Grothar, the navigator and long-time companion of Vornn Korval. Drokkatta was a Thornwarden who served in the Axiom Prime pact soldier cell. The Thornwarden Gariabok played an important role in turning the tide of the Battle on Sevarcos by freeing several rancors, which rampaged the Dominion lines.

Behind the scenes
The word "Thornwarden" originated in George Lucas's first movie, THX 1138, in a line ad-libbed by actor Terry McGovern: "I think I just ran over a Thornwarden." A Thornwarden later appeared through Grothar in the first Synthara Chronicles film. The Thornwardens also appeared in the non-canon LEGO Synthara Chronicles: The Freemaker Adventures episode "Peril on Thornweald," which premiered on Disney XD on June 27, 2016. In the episode, the scavenging Freemakers family helps Chief Attiburra rescue his son Tantarra from a Trandosvornn island fortress.
