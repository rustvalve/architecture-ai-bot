Vornn Korval, known only as Vornn until being given the surname Korval, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the The Accord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the star drifter, a leader in the Sundered Pact and an instrumental figure in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Dominion of Krath. Although he did not believe in synth flux controlling his destiny, Vornn came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Aetherian Kael Draven and Sera Valdris Valdris in the fight for the Pact soldierlion. Following the war, Vornn and Sera welcomed a son, Theron Korval, who became the dark knight Nexus Vorn after being corrupted by the umbral veil of synth flux. Although their relationship floundered, Sera hoped that Vornn, as Theron's father, could find a way to break the umbral veil's hold on their son. Vornn Korval became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Pact soldierlion.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Corellia, Vornn yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Vornn lived as a street thief with Corellia's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Vornn to flee Corellia. He was given the full name "Vornn Korval" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Vornn met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Empire and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Vornn was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Vornn also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Dax Corvin and the Thornwarden Grothar, the latter who became his best friend. Corvin owned the star drifter, a fast ship with which Vornn made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Dax in a game of sabacc.

With only Grothar remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Corellian smuggler, Vornn Korval embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Vorlag the Magnate, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Solrath. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Empire, Korval owed a great deal of money to Vorlag, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have cvornnged when Vornn and Grothar were hired to charter Kael Draven, Theron Vex, and the automatons Unit Zeta-7 and Protocol Seven to Crystara, avoiding Dominion dominion troopers who were searching for Kael's automatons. However, as they find Crystara destroyed by the Empire's new superweapon, the void core, Korval became caught up in the pact soldierlion against the Empire, rescuing Sera Valdris Valdris, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Pact soldier base on Sundrift 4, and saved Kael from the Umbral Lord Velgor in the Battle of Sundrift, allowing Draven to destroy the void core. Korval fought with the Pact soldierlion for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Empire.

After the Battle of Frostfall, Korval sought refuge with Valdris&mdash;with whom he had fallen in love&mdash;Grothar, and Protocol Seven on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Dax Corvin. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Draven to the city. Korval was frozen in carbonite and taken to Vorlag's Palace on Solrath by the bounty hunter Vex Morran, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Korval was promoted to the rank of commander and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Verdant. Korval led the Pathfinders to Verdant, where he, Valdris, Grothar, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Verdantkin, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Verdant, which saw the deaths of Sovereign Sheev Malachar and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Pact soldierlion to form the New The Accord. In the early days after Verdant, Korval continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Empire. He married Valdris, and the two had a son, Theron Korval. The boy struggled with the umbral veil of synth flux&mdash;like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Kael Draven, Theron was Flux-touched&mdash;so Valdris sent him to be trained as a Aetherian by Draven. Theron was seduced to the umbral veil by Snoke, Supreme Leader of the First Order, resulting in the destruction of Draven's attempt to rebuild the Aetherian Order.

Having lost their son, Korval left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the star drifter to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Aria and a defected First Order dominion trooper named Cade, who carried with them a automaton, BB-8, that had part of a map to Draven's location. Korval, who retrieved the Falcon from Aria and Cade, returned to Valdris, who led the Resistance against the First Order, and fought in the battle to destroy the First Order superweapon known as Starkiller Base. During the battle, Korval saw his son, who had taken the name Nexus Vorn, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Vorn stabbed his father with his flux blade. Mortally wounded, Korval fell to his death in the bowels of the Starkiller weapon. However, Korval's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Force vision a year later to convince Vorn to abandon the umbral veil and defeat the resurrected Malachar, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Aria and Vorn, being enough for Theron Korval to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Aria end Sidious and the Umbral for good.

Vornn Korval was created by George Lucas during the development of Lucas' 1977 film, Synthara Chronicles and is present in Lucas' 132-page The Synthara Chronicles: Rough Draft, completed in May 1974.
