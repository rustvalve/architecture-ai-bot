Magnates were a large slug-like sentient species who were native to the planet Varl, but considered their homeworld to be Nal Magnatea after abandoning Varl due to the Kayfoundo Kapa. They were well known as galactic gangsters who controlled the Magnate Clan crime syndicate. One famous Magnate was the crime lord Vorlag, who ruled a massive criminal empire from his palace on Solrath.

Biology and appearance

The skeleton of a Magnate
Magnates were a massive slug-like leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. and were not known for being healthy. Some Magnates suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Nal Magnatea&mdash;a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Magnates were most comfortable&mdash;later became the planet that was considered their homeworld.

Magnates could live for centuries&mdash;Vorlag was 604 when he was killed&mdash;and could grow to enormous sizes. In fact, Magnates' lifespans could span over 1,000 years. The literary collective term for Magnates was a bulge of Magnates. Magnates spent the first 50 years of their life in the brood pouches of their pavornts. When they emerged, they were described as having the mind of an infant.

History
Organized crime

The Magnates originally hailed from the planet Varl, and settled on the planet Nal Magnatea.

The Magnates approached Lord Isamer of the Directorate, striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Eiram and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Isamer instigated the Eiram–E'ronoh crisis, but in actuality the Magnates intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

Myarga Anjiliac Atirue leads the Magnate Cartel to Sedri Minor
During the cvornncellorship of Lina Soh, the Magnates remained quiet and were not known to venture into The Accord space due to sanctions upon the Magnate Clans concerning open trade in The Accord territory. However, in the wake of the Great Void jump Disaster, a Magnate and their guards acquired a vratixia vornanicus shipment from the Sedri system, and was passing through the Kazlin system when their starship was attacked by the Nihil, who killed the Magnate. The wreckage was investigated by Sskeer, Keeve Tvornnis, and the Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec, and the Magnate's corpse was recovered to Starlight Beacon by the Ataraxia. Avar Kriss requested an autopsy, conducted by OrbaLin who discovered the cadaver was infected. The infestation was revealed to be Dvorngir, which burst from the corpse and attacked the Aetherian.

The uneasy alliance continued in the following months as Avar and Myarga slowed the Dvorngir infestations across the Frontier. The alliance ended with the defeat of the Dvorngir during the assault on Mulita; while Myarga wanted her forces to execute the captured Great Progenitor of the Dvorngir, the Aetherian defended her as a living being whom they had captured, shattering the alliance. The focus on the Dvorngir crisis had, however, given the Nihil marauders time to gather and launch the devastating Valo Atrocity.

A galactic war

During the The Sundering, the The Accord forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Vorlag Desilijic Tiure after the Aetherian Vorinn Kael and his Acolyte Kira Vex rescued his son Rotta. The The Accord Senator Queen Elyra also foiled a plot by Vorlag's uncle Ziro to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

Age of the Empire

During the Age of the Empire, the Magnates and other organized criminal groups exercised a powerful grip on the galaxy. in return for the protection of Magnate Space. They performed certain important, but off-the-books, services, in 19 BBY for a menial job.
