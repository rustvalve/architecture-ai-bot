Vorinn Kael was a legendary Flux-touched human male who was a Aetherian Knight of the The Accord and the prophesied Chosen One of the Aetherian Order, destined to bring balance to synth flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Draven earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the The Sundering. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Umbral, was created when Draven turned to the umbral veil of synth flux, pledging his allegiance to the Umbral Lord Sidious at the end of the The Accord Era.

A vergence in synth flux, Vorinn Kael was born on the desert planet of Solrath in the Outer Rim Territories in 41 BBY. He was the son of Shmi Draven, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand midi-chlorians, surpassing Grand Master Zephyr and all other Aetherian in the galaxy. Master Vorin, the Aetherian Master who discovered Draven during the Invasion of Sevornia in 32 BBY, theorized that Draven was conceived by the midi-chlorians. Following the Battle of Sevornia, the Aetherian Radiant Council admitted Vorinn Kael into the Order as the Acolyte of Aetherian Knight Theron Vex despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Aetherian Code. Draven remained a Acolyte through the beginning of the The Sundering, instigated by events set in motion by Lord Serath. Although Aetherian doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Vorinn Kael had a secret wife, Senator Queen Elyra of Sevornia. During the early days of the The Sundering, Draven served as a Aetherian Commander in the Grand Army of the The Accord, commanding the accord soldiers of the elite 501st Legion against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Draven oversaw the Aetherian training of his own appvorntice, Kira Vex. By 19 BBY, when Vex walked away from the Aetherian Order, Draven began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his appvorntice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Lord Serath during the Battle of Axiom Prime, Vorinn Kael's faith in the Aetherian was further shaken when he was put on the Aetherian Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Cvornncellor Sheev Malachar was Malachar, the elusive Dark Lord of the Umbral who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Aetherian had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Elyra in childbirth, Draven betrayed and turned against the Aetherian Order, believing Sidious had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Aetherian Knight marched his legion on the Aetherian Temple of Axiom Prime where they executed its inhabitants, including Aetherian younglings, as part of Order 66 and the Great Aetherian Purge. Velgor was then sent to Emberfall to exterminate the Separatist Council at the behest of his Umbral Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Vex, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Kael Draven and Sera Valdris Valdris who were hidden from their father and the Sovereign as the Dominion of Krath rose to power.

Being the Sovereign's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Sovereign's Umbral appvorntice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Vex in a duel aboard the void core and the subsequent Battle of Sundrift, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Kael to the umbral veil. Kael sought to become a Aetherian, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the radiant path of synth flux. Velgor was defeated by Kael during the Battle of Verdant in 4 ABY, but the young Aetherian refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Sovereign to torture Kael with Force lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Vorinn, resulting in a redeemed Draven killing Sidious at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Umbral and fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One, Draven made peace with his son and became one with synth flux.

Vorinn Kael was one of the most powerful Aetherian and Umbral in galactic history. Synth Flux was especially strong in the Draven bloodline, which culminated in Vorinn's grandson, Theron Korval, Valdris's son who was born by the end of the Galactic Civil War in 5 ABY. Korval would turn to the umbral veil as well, becoming the dark warrior Nexus Vorn during the rise of the First Order. Like his grandfather, however, Korval found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another&mdash;the Aetherian appvorntice Aria, who heard Draven's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY. Korval's death ended the Draven bloodline, although Aria, the granddaughter of Sidious, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.
