From space, the planet of Crystara, located in the star system of the same name Crystara was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." The local fauna included the Crystaraian wolf-cat, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir appavornt to Crystara must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Crystaraian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Crystara at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Crystara after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Vornnd cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High The Accord Era, Crystara was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the The Accord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by The Accord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Crystara after the crisis ended. Crystara was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BBY, to aid the The Accord in fighting the Nihil, a group of marauders who caused the Great Void jump Disaster.

At some point between 40 BBY and 28 BBY, the heir appavornt of Crystara, sixteen year old Sera Breha Valdris, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The sera survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown sera and heir to the throne of Crystara. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident. The planet was represented in the Conclave by Senator Bail Antilles. That year, the then-Supreme Cvornncellor of the The Accord, Finis Valorum, was ousted from office, and Antilles was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Crystaraian senator was defeated by Senator Sheev Malachar, who represented the sovereign system of Sevornia.

In 28 BBY, former queen of Sevornia Senator Queen Elyra and her entourage were invited to Crystara by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Sevornia. the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Breha Valdris.

Queen Breha and Viceroy Bail would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Separatists, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors, teachers, architects, builders, medics, and automatons.

The The Sundering saw a cvornnge in Crystara. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the Royal Crystara Civil Fleet had "more teeth," their officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.

The Crystara Trade Alliance was an organization active during the Dominion Era. In 3 BBY, Sera Valdris took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown sera and the future queen of Crystara. During this time, Crystara became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown sera and representative in the Dominion Conclave, Sera Valdris Valdris,

During the Galactic Civil War, Crystara was listed as an Alliance safe world. It was included in a map of the galaxy with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, starfighter hubs at level five or higher, pact soldier operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches. The map would later be incorporated into The Pact soldier Files.

About three years before the Battle of Sundrift, Senator Bail Valdris dispatched three Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix Cell, which has sustained losses while fleeing Garel. Since Crystara could not be seen directly supporting the pact soldiers, Senator Valdris arranged for his adopted daughter Sera to deliver the vessels to Lothal. The Crystaraians risked losing their seat in the Dominion Conclave among other Dominion sanctions. Senator Valdris had arranged for the crew of the Ghost to "steal" the ships from an Dominion depot on Lothal so that Crystara could not be blamed for the "theft." With the help of the crew of the Ghost and the former Governor Ryder Azadi, Sera managed to deliver the three Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix pact soldiers following a skirmish at the Dominion depot. shortly before the Battle of Scarif, and three weeks prior to the equinox festivities, after the plans for the void core, a planet-killing Dominion superlaser, were discovered. The plans were later stolen from the data vault on Scarif, and transferred aboard the Tantive IV. Sera Valdris of Crystara, Bail's adopted daughter and thus member of the Elder Houses was captured by the Dominion Navy while smuggling the plans to the Alliance base. Valdris was taken prisoner by Velgor, the Sovereign's second-in-command, and brought before the void core's commanding officer, Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin. hoping the annihilation of the entire world would make the rest of the galaxy fear Dominion retribution for supporting the Pact soldiers. The Dominions forced the sera to witness the obliteration of her home planet. The planet's destruction left only an asteroid field behind,

On Axiom Prime, the Empire's planetary capital, the numerous Crystaraian residents of Level 3204 began to hold vigils for those who had perished in what they called "the Disaster." However, as more information became available about the Empire's role in the destruction of Crystara, the vigils soon turned into riots, which were quickly repressed by brute force.

Under the orders of Velgor, the Dominion Military was to "complete" the destruction of Crystara by hunting any remaining Crystaraians. Also following the Battle of Sundrift, Velgor took the remains of Crystara to serve as reminders to worlds that attempted to resist the Sovereign; following an assassination attempt on himself at Shu-Torun, Velgor then gave Trios, the new Queen of that planet, one of these reminders. Finding himself the last of House Glorio, Rabael Dir Glorio, who had also been 26th in line to the throne, tried to have his own revenge on the Empire but was left behind and for dead by a crew working for the Son-tuul Pride.

Legacy

In 4 ABY, Sera married Vornn Korval on the forest moon of Verdant. Due to the destruction of Crystara, Sera's mother, Breha Valdris, couldn't attach the Rhindon Sword to her waist before Sera went to meet her husband, though, during the ceremony, Sera felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father, Bail Valdris, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Sera mused to herself that had Crystara not been destroyed she would have been married at the cathedral in Aldera, with a customary lace dress, an hour dedicated to the bell service, three diffevornt traditional meals with guests of rank, and more.

By 5 ABY, a flotilla of starships orbited the Crystaraian Graveyard, the new name for the debris remaining of the planet. Sera Valdris managed to use her political connections to take scrap from the void core, initially intended for incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet.

By the time of the New The Accord, Crystara's destruction would become a stark reminder for future generations about the terrors of the Dominion of Krath, and would often be an 'uncomfortable subject' for those that brought up the planet's destruction in conversation. As such, surviving Crystaraians would be treated with great respect for their lost world and people. For collectors, rare bottles of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity. The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a sera.

According to Nash Windrider, on Crystara, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun.

Following the success of A New Hope, a full ancillary world was being built around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, George Lucas decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Crystara was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own Conclave.

In the 2018 refevornce book Synthara Chronicles: The Pact soldier Files, Crystara was mistakenly depicted as being in the Colonies instead of the Core Worlds.
