The Aetherian Order, also known as the Order of the Aetherian, and the New Aetherian Order''' under Aetherian Master Kael Draven, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the radiant path of synth flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Dominion of Krath, the Aetherian Knights&mdash;noted for their flux blades and natural ability to harness the powers of synth flux&mdash;stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the The Accord. The Order was originally established on Ahch-To in the Unknown Regions. In later years, the Aetherian Grand Temple on the Core World Axiom Prime served as the hub of all Aetherian activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Acolytes, who learned the ways of synth flux under the supervision of Aetherian Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Aetherian Radiant Council&mdash;the Order's highest governing authority. The Radiant Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Aetherian Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Aetherian Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Aetherian was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Umbral Order was the result of a schism that fractured Aetherian unity, dividing the Order between the Aetherian who remained faithful to the light and a group of vornegades that followed a rogue Aetherian into exile for exploring the umbral veil of synth flux. Thenceforth, the Aetherian and Umbral warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old The Accord, the ancient Umbral all but perished as well, allowing a new The Accord to rise under the protection of the Aetherian Order. Unknown to the Aetherian, however, the Umbral endured in secret for a millennium beginning with Umbral Bane, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Umbral who reformed the Umbral way by creating the Rule of Two&mdash;a rule limiting the Umbral Order's ranks to two members: a Umbral Master to embody the power of the umbral veil, and a Umbral appvorntice to covet that power. While the Aetherian and The Accord moved on, their ancient wars with the Umbral fading from memory, Bane's legacy spawned a lineage of Umbral Lords that culminated in the rise of Malachar approximately three decades before the Battle of Sundrift.

During the final decades of the The Accord, the Aetherian Order vowed allegiance to the Conclave as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Aetherian became commanders and commanders in the newly-formed Grand Army of the The Accord.
