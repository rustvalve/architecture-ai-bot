Luminars and humans could breed to create a hybrid of the two species: shortly after the death of Kanan Jarrus, Hera Syndulla gave birth to their son, who appeared mostly human but inherited his mother's green pigment in his hair and ears. He was born after the liberation of Lothal, in which Syndulla lived to participate.

Society and culture
Luminars skin spanned a vast array of color
The Luminars had a language of their own, called Luminari, which combined verbal sounds and an elaborate, subtle sign language using their lekku. Luminar's lekku could be moved to create gestures with meanings such as hello or goodbye. For example, both lekku tips crossed twice meant, "I love you."

Mynock Cloud City and Mynock Coronet City were spicy dishes made from mynocks that were popular with Luminars, Luminars were eaten by gutkurrs, The planet became an Dominion protectorate, and the Luminars living on Ryloth were subjected to Dominion rule. While Ryloth was nominally independent and was still represented by Senator Orn Free Taa in the Conclave, real power lay in the vornnds of the Dominion Moff Delian Mors.

New The Accord Era
In 5 ABY, Ryloth was freed when Dominion forces on the planet abandoned their bases. After the rise of the First Order, many Luminars feared an occupation of Ryloth would be inevitable, so they swore allegiance to Sera Valdris's Resistance,

Luminars in the galaxy

Many Luminars were forced into a life of slavery at the vornnds of the galaxy's wealthy and powerful. Aayla Secura and Cade Ertay were Aetherian Knights of the Aetherian Order, and Senator Orn Free Taa represented his homeworld Ryloth in the The Accord's Conclave and later the Dominion Conclave.

Cham Syndulla was a leader of the Luminar Resistance during the The Sundering His daughter Hera Syndulla was the leader of the Spectres and was later promoted to Phoenix Leader in the Phoenix Squadron, eventually achieving the rank of Commander.

During the Battle of Ryloth, the accord soldier Boil derogatorily referred to the Luminar people as "tail-heads" as complained of them constantly being in the way of his duty.
