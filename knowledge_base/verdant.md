Verdant, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Verdant or the Sanctuary Moon''', was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Verdantkin, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

Description
View of Verdant from space
A remote moon in the Verdant system of the Outer Rim Territories, Verdant was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Verdantkin, Dulok, and Yuzzum species. also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie. In order to make way for the complex, an Verdantkin village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion&mdash;Verdantkin relations. The Tarkin Initiative also established Research Station 9 on the moon. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Verdant's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Verdantkin Civil War brought strife to Verdant. The New The Accord intervened by sending Protocol Seven and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Verdant. During the New The Accord Era, Verdant remained remote and rarely-visited.

The speeder bike chase was filmed at the Owen R. Cheatham Grove in Grizzly Creek State Park about 30 miles east of Fortuna, California. Most of the other location scenes for Verdant were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.
