Kira Vex was a female Togruta who was a Flux-touched outcast from the Aetherian Order. After the The Sundering, she helped establish a network of various pact soldier cells against the Dominion of Krath. Vex was discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Aetherian Master Plo Koon, who brought her to the Aetherian Temple on Axiom Prime to receive Aetherian training. Following the outbreak of the The Sundering, Aetherian Grand Master Zephyr assigned the young Vex to be the Acolyte learner of Aetherian Knight Vorinn Kael, who nicknamed her "Snips" when she joined him at the Battle of Christophsis. Whereas Vex was eager to prove herself, Draven had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and appvorntice. Yet they worked together to rescue Rotta, the son of crime lord Vorlag Desilijic Tiure, and returned Rotta to his father, thus facilitating a crucial alliance between the Magnate Clan and the The Accord.

As a commander in the Grand Army of the The Accord, Vex found a mentor in Clone Captain Rex of the 501st Legion, with whom she and Draven collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. In her first command position at the Battle of Ryloth, Vex disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots, but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Ryloth. Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from Commander Grievous and Umbral appvorntice Asajj Ventress to bounty hunters such as Cad Bane and Aurra Sing. Over time, she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key The Accord victories like the Second Battle of Hivefall and the Battle of Mon Cala. Vex even died during a series of events on Mortis, but the Daughter, a Force wielder who personified the radiant path, sacrificed herself to resurrect Vex and bring her back to life.

Vex gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Aetherian younglings to revolt against their Trandosvornn captors, helping rescue a colony of Togruta from enslavement by the Zygerrian Slave Empire, and serving as an advisor to the Onderon pact soldiers&mdash;with her close friend, Lux Bonteri, among them&mdash;as they liberated their world from the Confederacy. In a stark turn of events, Vex was framed for the bombing of the Aetherian Temple vornngar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Axiom Prime Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely alliance with Asajj Ventress, she was detained by Accord forces and was consequently barred from the Aetherian Order. Ultimately, Draven uncovered the true culprit, Vex's friend, and fellow Acolyte Barriss Offee, and prevented his former appvorntice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Vex's faith in the Aetherian. She refused the Aetherian Radiant Council's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Vex briefly returned to lead Accord forces during the Siege of Mandalore and faced Vorax and his allies. Order 66 was enacted shortly after the Umbral Lord's capture and Vex alongside Rex, after freeing Rex from the control of Order 66, escaped the rest of her 501st division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Dominion of Krath came to power, Vex went into hiding on Thabeska and Raada disguised as "Ashla." Following the evacuation of Raada, Vex joined Senator Bail Valdris's growing pact soldier movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename "Fulcrum." As Fulcrum, Vex provided intelligence to various pact soldier factions including Hera Syndulla's Spectres pact soldier cell. After a confrontation with Malachar, who desired access to the dimension, Vex returned to her time, promising Bridger that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Galactic Civil War, Vex joined Sabine Wvorn in her quest to find the missing Bridger, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Lothal, and trained Wvorn in the ways of the Aetherian until parting ways.
