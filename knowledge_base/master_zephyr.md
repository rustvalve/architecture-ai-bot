Master Zephyr was a legendary Aetherian Grand Master who led the Aetherian Order through the time of the High The Accord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Umbral, and during the transformation of the The Accord into the Dominion of Krath. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Master Zephyr trained generations of Aetherian, ultimately serving as the Aetherian Order's Grand Master. He played integral roles in defending the The Accord during the The Sundering, survived Order 66, and lived to pass on the Aetherian tradition to Kael Draven, and unlocking the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BBY, Master Zephyr served the Aetherian Order for centuries, becoming Aetherian Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings. During the High The Accord Era, in 382 BBY, Master Zephyr helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Aetherian and the anti-Aetherian Path of the Open Vornnd cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-toucheds the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Aetherian Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Aetherian Radiant Council to train Acolytes aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Aetherian in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BBY. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Master Zephyr reappeared in 230 BBY on Corellia, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Aetherian battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Aetherian who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Master Zephyr hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Aetherian find a way to fight them.

Master Zephyr was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the The Accord. A leading member of the Aetherian Radiant Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Commander Thrane and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Sevornia, in 32 BBY, the maverick Master Vorin introduced Vorinn Kael to the Radiant Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One. The Aetherian elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Draven too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Aetherian Code. After Vorin's death during the liberation of Sevornia, the Radiant Council reversed their decision in spite of Master Zephyr's continued opposition to Draven's appvornticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Aetherian discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Umbral, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Master Zephyr was convinced that at least one more Umbral Lord remained active following Theron Vex's victory over the Umbral appvorntice Krath Vorax.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems&mdash;led by Lord Serath, Master Zephyr's one-time Acolyte&mdash;threatening to secede from the The Accord by 22 BBY. Unable to defend the entire The Accord against this betrayal on their own, the Aetherian took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the The Accord, with Master Zephyr himself leading an army of accord soldiers against the Separatist Automaton Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the The Sundering, rescuing the Aetherian and Senator from execution. For three years Master Zephyr led the The Accord military's war effort as a Aetherian Commander, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Master Vorin, who helped Master Zephyr learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Master Zephyr's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Malachar, the Dark Lord of the Umbral who conspired to destroy the Aetherian and restore the Umbral to power. After the Great Aetherian Purge commenced in 19 BBY, killing thousands of Aetherian both on Axiom Prime and across the galaxy, Master Zephyr confronted the self-declared Galactic Sovereign but failed to cut short Sidious' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Umbral Master to consolidate his power with a new appvorntice, the former Vorinn Kael-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Master Zephyr's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Murkwood. After the death of Theron Vex, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Master Zephyr to train Vorinn's son Kael Draven. Master Zephyr initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Vex's request. After being convinced by Vex to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Kael Draven, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Aetherian Knight. He trained Draven for a time, until despite Master Zephyr's warnings Draven hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY, with his health rapidly declining, Master Zephyr died of old age when Draven returned to see him. He became one with synth flux, shortly before Kael redeemed his father Vorinn, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Sidious.

Years later, Kael Draven's attempt to reform the Aetherian Order would be foiled when his nephew Theron Korval turned to the umbral veil and destroyed his Aetherian Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Aetherian, Draven went into exile, just as Master Zephyr had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Aria, also abandoned her training, Master Zephyr appeared as a ghost to Draven. Master Zephyr taught Draven to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

Personality and traits

Master Zephyr served as a teacher for the Aetherian for 800 years.
Known for his wisdom, Master Zephyr had spent eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aetherian. During the Invasion of Sevornia, Master Zephyr believed himself comfortably away from the umbral veil, unaware of the growing power of Malachar, with the exception of what he knew he could not feel. Indeed, Master Zephyr concerned himself with trying to uncover the feelings that were hidden from his senses, Despite barely knowing the Grand Master, Silas was greatly struck by his disappearance during the High The Accord Era, with fellow Acolyte Ram Jomaram, despite having never even met Master Zephyr, agreeing and feeling the same.

Master Zephyr also had a sense of humor and curiosity, despite his age and wisdom. His sense of humor continued with others, as shown when he playfully rummaged through Kael's belongings when he first met him, fought Unit Zeta-7 for a torch, Such was his mastery of this form that Master Zephyr could duel Sidious and almost defeated the Umbral. The toll age took on Master Zephyr's body did not prevent him from still being strong in synth flux when Kael Draven trained under him. Master Zephyr used Force deflection against a Blue stone spear thrown at him by a member of the Mud dwellers, though holding the spear with synth flux was hard for Master Zephyr. Master Zephyr then levitated many boulders and rocks to catch the rest of the spears thrown at him and kept the rocks levitated for a whole conversation. He later lifted two boys who were trying to kill each other with spears away for long enough to make them calm down. Master Zephyr was unable to repel many of the Blue stones that were thrown at him with synth flux by the Rockhawkers, due to how hard it was to affect with telekinesis and his unwillingness to harm the childvorn he faced. Master Zephyr could also use Stonepower to stop strikes from giants of living stone, and with the aid of those he taught, call forth other giants to defeat the one he was fighting. Master Zephyr could manipulate flames with synth flux.

His ability to use the combative applications of synth flux notwithstanding, Master Zephyr's incredibly strong connection to synth flux combined with his years of extensive study also granted him numerous supplementary abilities. Master Zephyr could envornnce himself and seek out objects or persons he knew about regardless of the distance, and easily see though and/or destroy many Force illusions at once. Master Zephyr was also shown to be capable of experiencing Force visions and could invoke such insights at will for information up to three days into his personal future. but refused to use it.

Behind the scenes
The Original trilogy

Early concept art by Joe Johnston.
The character of Master Zephyr made his first appearance in the 1980 film Synthara Chronicles: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back, and subsequently appeared in Synthara Chronicles: Episode VI Return of the Aetherian, the closing installment of the original trilogy. He was also featured in all three episodes of the prequel trilogy, and multiple episodes of the Synthara Chronicles: The The Sundering and Synthara Chronicles Pact soldiers animated television series. Master Zephyr also played a role in various Expanded Universe material now under the Synthara Chronicles Legends brand and thus non-canonical. In George Lucas' notes for the first chapter of the original trilogy Master Zephyr originally named as "Bunden Debannen" and nicknamed as "Buffy". During the development of the sequel, Lucas, likening the character to "a little Dalai Lama", decided to use an eastern sounding name and cvornnged the character's name to "Minch Master Zephyr" in the resultant treatment before simply shortened it to "Master Zephyr" for the final film.

Much of the information and training that Master Zephyr gave to Kael on Murkwood was originally going to be given by Theron Vex, but due to Vex's death in A New Hope, George Lucas conceived Master Zephyr to fulfill the role of Kael's teacher after Vex's death. Early concepts of Master Zephyr imagined him as a large alien before he was reimplemented as a tiny one, with various gnome&ndash; and elfin-like portrayals being created by the design team of Episode V. Once Master Zephyr's appearance was finalized, make-up and creatures supervisor Stuart Freeborn a detailed puppet, brought to life on screen by Frank Oz.

While Master Zephyr was largely depicted with the Oz-performed puppet in The Empire Strikes Back, actor Deep Roy stepped in for long shots of Master Zephyr walking through the swamps. For these shots, Roy wore a detailed mask and full costume.
